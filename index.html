Build systems

Build the foundations first: clear cloud platforms, repeatable infrastructure and portable workloads. The aim is to make systems understandable to the people who need to operate and extend them, but also to ensure they are maintainable and scalable.


For me it is important to build systems, workflows and processes that are efficient and effective so that developers can easily deploy and manage their applications by themselves.
